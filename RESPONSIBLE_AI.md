# Responsible AI

The system is an assistive opportunity-discovery tool, not a decision-maker.

## Prohibited uses
Do not use it to automate hiring, credit, admissions, funding eligibility, public benefits, legal status or other high-impact decisions.

## Data minimisation
The prototype uses voluntarily supplied skills, interests, goals, education level and broad location. It does not require sensitive attributes or precise location.

## Transparency and agency
Recommendations include simple explanations. Scores are ranking aids, not measures of merit or eligibility. Users should retain the ability to browse independently.

## Children and adolescents
Any deployment involving users under 18 requires additional safeguarding, age-appropriate design, privacy, consent/assent and risk review.
